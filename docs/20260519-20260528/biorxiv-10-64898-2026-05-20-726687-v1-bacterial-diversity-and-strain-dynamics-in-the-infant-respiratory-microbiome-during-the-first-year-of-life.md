## 摘要
背景：人体微生物定植始于出生后即刻，每个身体部位形成独特的生态位。虽然早期肠道微生物动态及其与儿童健康的关系已被广泛研究，但婴儿早期呼吸系统的细菌定植仍知之甚少，尤其是在物种和菌株水平。

结果：在这里，我们对西澳大利亚儿童哮喘研究（CAS）中58名健康婴儿的鼻咽样本进行了纵向收集（2、6和12月龄），并从六个优势菌属中分离出925株菌株进行全基因组测序和分析。我们的结果扩展了基因组参考目录，并揭示了婴儿气道优势类群中显著的菌株水平多样性。板扫宏基因组学识别出四个年龄依赖性的微生物组谱群（MPGs），确认了先前的观察结果，同时实现了高分辨率的物种和菌株分析。群落成熟的特征是从早期的金黄色葡萄球菌优势转变为12月龄时卡他莫拉菌优势增加，同时伴随流行率和队列水平菌株多样性的显著时间变化。

结论：这些发现解析了婴儿鼻咽微生物群在物种和菌株水平的组成，揭示了类群特异的定植模式。通过大幅扩展公开可用的代表性不足的气道类群参考基因组，这项工作也为早期呼吸道微生物群和呼吸结局的功能后续研究奠定了基础。

## Abstract
BackgroundMicrobial colonisation of the human body begins immediately after birth, with each body site forming a distinct ecological niche. While early gut microbial dynamics and their links to paediatric health have been considerably studied, the bacterial colonisation of the early infant respiratory system remains poorly understood, particularly at the species and strain level.

ResultsHere, we generated and analysed whole genome sequencing of 925 isolates from six dominant genera in nasopharyngeal samples from 58 healthy infants enrolled in the Childhood Asthma Study (CAS) in Western Australia, collected longitudinally from birth (2, 6, and 12 months old). Our results expanded genomic reference catalogues and uncovered substantial strain-level diversity in dominant infant airway taxa. Plate-sweep metagenomics identified four microbiome profile groups (MPGs) with age-dependent membership, confirming prior observations while enabling high-resolution species and strain analyses. Community maturation was characterised by a shift from early Staphylococcus aureus dominance to increased Moraxella catarrhalis dominance by 12 months of age, alongside marked temporal changes in prevalence and cohort-level strain diversity.

ConclusionsThese findings resolve infant nasopharyngeal microbiota composition at the species and strain level, revealing taxon-specific colonisation patterns. By substantially expanding publicly available reference genomes for underrepresented airway taxa, this work also provides a foundation for functional follow-up studies of the early respiratory microbiota and respiratory outcomes.
