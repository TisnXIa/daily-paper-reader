## 摘要
细菌胞外囊泡（bEVs）使肠道微生物能够将生物活性货物递送至宿主细胞，但特定细菌产生者尚未被系统识别。在此，我们利用宏蛋白质组学分析对健康个体粪便来源的bEVs进行剖析，揭示囊泡形成在肠道细菌门中广泛存在。我们选取了一组形成囊泡的菌种，显示bEVs在体内定位于不同组织，包括肺、肝、肾和骨等肠外部位，提示其在胃肠道之外的作用。我们对内吞不同菌种bEVs后的肠上皮细胞和巨噬细胞进行分析，发现了显著且细胞类型特异性的反应。例如，脆弱拟杆菌bEVs促进抗炎线粒体-端粒调控，而一组共生菌来源的bEVs则促进上皮存活和结构更新。通过将蛋白质组、脂质组和代谢组货物谱分析与转录输出相结合，我们发现特定拟杆菌门来源的蛋白货物可激活细胞保护性应激防御，同时减弱炎症信号。这些发现共同构建了bEV组成、摄取和宿主反应的多层次比较图谱，为理解bEV介导的微生物组-宿主交流提供了框架。

## Abstract
Bacterial extracellular vesicles (bEVs) enable gut microbiota to deliver bioactive cargo to host cells, yet the specific bacterial producers have not been systematically identified. Here, we profiled stool-derived bEVs from healthy individuals using metaproteomic profiling, revealing that vesiculation is widespread across gut bacterial phyla. We selected a subset of vesiculating species, showing that bEVs localize to distinct tissues in vivo, including extraintestinal sites such as lung, liver, kidney, and bone, suggesting roles beyond the gastrointestinal tract. We profiled intestinal epithelial cells and macrophages after endocytosing bEVs from various species, uncovering pronounced, cell-type-specific responses. For example, Bacteroides fragilis bEVs promote anti-inflammatory mitochondrial-telomeric regulation, while a set of commensal-derived bEVs contribute to epithelial survival and structural renewal. By combining proteomic, lipidomic, and metabolomic cargo profiling with transcriptional output, we find that specific Bacteroidota-derived protein cargo activates cytoprotective stress defenses while attenuating inflammatory signaling. Together, these findings establish a multi-layered comparative atlas of bEV composition, uptake, and host response, providing a framework for understanding bEV-mediated microbiome-host communication.
