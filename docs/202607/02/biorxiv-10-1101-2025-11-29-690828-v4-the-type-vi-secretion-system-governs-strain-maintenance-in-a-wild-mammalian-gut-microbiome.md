## 摘要
哺乳动物肠道中的细菌以密集群落共存，其中接触依赖性拮抗机制广泛存在。VI型分泌系统（T6SS）是一种在肠道拟杆菌目中普遍存在的细菌间毒素输送途径，但其在自然进化微生物组中的功能仍知之甚少。在此，我们研究了来自野生小鼠（WildR微生物组）的生理相关肠道群落中拟杆菌属中T6SS的作用。利用新开发的遗传工具和在WildR群落内进行菌株功能替代的策略，我们证明了WildR分离株酸梭菌（B. acidifaciens）利用T6SS拮抗共存的拟杆菌目成员。我们还发现，T6SS功能的丧失会损害酸梭菌在群落中的长期维持，但不会影响其初始定植，从而确立该系统为菌株持久性的决定因素。我们鉴定的T6SS位于一个整合性接合元件（ICE）上。靶向测序方法ICE-seq揭示，T6SS-ICE分布在WildR微生物组中选择性的拟杆菌目和Muribaculaceae物种之间，并且似乎在近期发生了交换。我们还证明，将T6SS-ICE转移至WildR分离株Phocaeicola vulgatus可在小鼠中赋予短暂的定植优势，但与最终的种群下降有关。我们的发现表明，T6SS可以稳定复杂、共进化的肠道微生物组中特定菌株的存在，但其价值取决于环境，并受到宿主群落的生态和生理景观的制约。

## Abstract
Bacteria inhabiting the mammalian gut coexist in dense communities where contact-dependent antagonism mechanisms are widespread. The type VI secretion system (T6SS) is an interbacterial toxin delivery pathway prevalent among gut Bacteroidales, yet its function in naturally evolved microbiomes remains poorly defined. Here, we examine the role of the T6SS in Bacteroides within a physiologically relevant gut community derived from wild mice (the WildR microbiome). Using newly developed genetic tools and a strategy for functional replacement of strains within the WildR community, we demonstrate that the WildR isolate B. acidifaciens employs a T6SS to antagonize co-resident Bacteroidales. We also show that loss of T6SS function compromises the long-term maintenance of B. acidifaciens in the community but not its initial colonization, establishing the system as a determinant of strain persistence. The T6SS we identified resides on an integrative and conjugative element (ICE). ICE-seq, a targeted sequencing approach, reveals that the T6SS-ICE is distributed among select Bacteroidales and Muribaculaceae species in the WildR microbiome, between which it appears to be recently exchanged. We also show that transfer of the T6SS-ICE to WildR isolate Phocaeicola vulgatus confers transient colonization benefits in mice, but is linked to eventual population decline. Our findings demonstrate that the T6SS can stabilize the presence of specific strains within a complex, co-evolved gut microbiome, yet its value is context dependent and constrained by the ecological and physiological landscape of the host community.
