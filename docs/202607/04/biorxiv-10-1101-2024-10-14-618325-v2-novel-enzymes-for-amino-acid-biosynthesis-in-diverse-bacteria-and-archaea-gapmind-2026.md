## 摘要
我们之前描述了GapMind，一个用于注释细菌和古菌基因组中氨基酸生物合成途径的自动化网络工具。我们使用GapMind识别生物合成途径中的缺口，并系统地利用比较基因组学和高通量遗传学来识别候选基因以填补这些缺口。我们通过跨物种互补实验确认了其中十种提议酶的活性。亮点包括一种新的甘氨酸合成途径、两个可替代磷酸丝氨酸磷酸酶的家族、一种替代的N-琥珀酰-L,L-二氨基庚二酸脱琥珀酰酶、一种替代的N-乙酰鸟氨酸脱乙酰酶，以及一种双功能的MetB/MetC。我们更新了GapMind以包含这些额外的酶。在208个具有高质量基因组且能在最低培养基中生长的原核生物中，氨基酸生物合成中无法解释的缺失步骤或缺口的平均数量从每个基因组1.4个下降到0.7个。剩余缺口主要涉及磷酸基团的获得或丢失。

## Abstract
We previously described GapMind, an automated web-based tool for annotating amino acid biosynthesis pathways in bacterial and archaeal genomes. We used GapMind to identify gaps in biosynthetic pathways and systematically used comparative genomics and high-throughput genetics to identify candidate genes to fill these gaps. We confirmed the activity of ten of the proposed enzymes by using cross-species complementation assays. Highlights include a novel route to glycine, two families that can replace phosphoserine phosphatase, an alternative N-succinyl-L,L-diaminopimelate desuccinylase, an alternative N-acetylornithine deacetylase, and a bifunctional MetB/MetC. We updated GapMind to include these additional enzymes. Across 208 prokaryotes that have high-quality genomes and can grow in minimal media, the average number of unexplained missing steps or gaps in amino acid biosynthesis dropped from 1.4 per genome to 0.7 per genome. The majority of remaining gaps involve the gain or loss of phosphate groups.
