## 摘要
土壤微生物对植物的生存和生产力至关重要，但控制不同地区根际招募的因素尚不清楚。本研究利用精英品种在七个地点-年份试验中调查了大麦根际微生物组，评估环境因素和作物基因型对细菌和真菌群落组成的影响。三个地点位于美国北部大平原，夏威夷作为对比环境。温室交互移植实验确定了土壤理化因素和土壤接种物对根际群落结构的相对贡献。利用16S和ITS2扩增子测序，研究表征了细菌和真菌微生物组，并评估了环境、土壤化学和大麦遗传对微生物群落组装的贡献。在大麦适应范围内的地点，放线菌门是优势门，而夏威夷则以变形菌门为主。方差分解显示，73%的细菌和80%的真菌属与地点-年份效应相关，而53%的细菌和36%的真菌属对土壤因素有响应。富集分析发现，21.6%的细菌和51.4%的真菌ASV是大麦特定遗传亚群特有的。交互移植实验的结果验证了田间观察，表明20.7%的群落结构变异由土壤解释，而18.2%由接种物来源解释。这些发现表明，环境变异是根际群落组成的主要约束，但在这些约束内，大麦基因型驱动了独特细菌和真菌类群的招募。

## Abstract
Soil microorganisms are crucial for plant survival and productivity, but factors governing rhizosphere recruitment across diverse regions remain unclear. This study investigated the rhizosphere microbiome of barley, using elite cultivars across seven location-year trials to evaluate the effects of environmental factors and crop genotype on bacterial and fungal community composition. Three locations were in the US northern Great Plains, and Hawai'i was used as a contrasting environment. A greenhouse reciprocal transplant study determined the relative contributions of soil physicochemical factors and soil inoculum to rhizosphere community structure. Using 16S and ITS2 amplicon sequencing, the study characterized bacterial and fungal microbiomes and assessed the contribution of environment, soil chemistry, and barley genetics to microbial community assembly. In locations within the adapted range of barley, Actinobacteriota was the dominant phylum, while Proteobacteria was dominant in Hawai'i. Variance partitioning showed that 73% of bacterial and 80% of fungal genera were associated with location-year effects while 53% of bacterial and 36% of fungal genera were responsive to soil factors. Enrichment analysis found 21.6% of bacterial and 51.4% of fungal ASVs were unique to specific barley genetic subpopulations. Results from the reciprocal transplant study validated field observations by demonstrating that 20.7% of the variation in community structure was explained by soil while 18.2% was explained by inoculum source. These findings demonstrate that environmental variation is the dominant constraint on rhizosphere community composition but within these constraints, barley genotype drives recruitment of distinct bacterial and fungal taxa.

### 8. 不足与局限
- **仅用扩增子测序**：无法获得物种或菌株水平的精细分类，功能预测（FAPROTAX）精度有限；缺乏宏基因组/宏转录组验证。

- **气候因素未直接纳入方差分解**：文中承认由于降水和温度在地点-年份间完全共线，无法在模型中单独分离其贡献。
- **真菌数据不完整**：swMT 2021年真菌数据因测序质量被剔除，导致该地点-年份的真菌分析缺失。
