## 摘要
微生物群落遵循普遍的宏观生态学尺度定律，但哪些子过程产生这些定律在不同竞争理论框架下仍存在争议。在这里，我们将一个修正的Yule-Simon模型校准到来自11个不同微生物环境的宏基因组数据，揭示微生物生态系统占据了一个定性独特的二维参数机制空间区域，其特征是近中性招募（即线性优先连接）和广泛的多样化策略，不同于任何先前研究的复杂系统，包括原核生物蛋白质组和城市经济。这一独特位置，经过分析确认并通过稀疏数据稳健性测试验证，为观察到的微生物等级-频率分布中的自相似性提供了机制解释，也为生态组装提供了定量标志。

## Abstract
Microbial communities obey universal macroecological scaling laws, but which sub-processes generate them remains debated across competing theoretical frameworks. Here we calibrate a modified Yule-Simon model to metagenomic data from 11 distinct microbial environments, revealing that microbial ecosystems occupy a qualitatively distinct region of a two-parameter mechanistic space, characterized by near-neutral recruitment (i.e., linear preferential attachment) and broad diversification strategies, unlike any previously studied complex system, including prokaryotic proteomes and urban economies. This distinctive position, confirmed analytically and validated against sparse-data robustness tests, provides both a mechanistic explanation for observed self-similarities in microbial rank-frequency distributions and a quantitative signature of ecological assembly.
