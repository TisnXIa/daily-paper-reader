## 摘要
多核藻类是进行核分裂而不进行胞质分裂的生物，从而形成多核细胞。大多数关于多核藻类细菌群落的研究采用16S rRNA测序，主要关注绿色多核藻类的羽藻目。近年来的研究分析了来自多个藻类分类群（如绿藻门、褐藻门和红藻门）的宏基因组组装基因组（MAGs），揭示了更多关于细菌生物合成机制和潜在共生关系的信息。鉴于多核藻类（如羽藻属、蕨藻属、松藻属以及黄绿色藻类无隔藻属）的世界性分布及其独特的形态，有必要更好地了解其相关的细菌群落。为此，使用Illumina NovaSeq仪器对Vaucheria bursata LB2067的丝状体进行测序，并筛选了所有公开可用的多核藻类短读长和长读长数据集，以恢复MAGs。所有从羽藻目和无隔藻目恢复的MAGs在门水平上均显示出假单胞菌门的高度优势，但在目或科水平上没有一致的模式。与非多核藻类相比，来自多核藻类目的MAGs普遍具有特定KEGG通路（如双向聚磷酸盐代谢和核黄素生物合成）的高完整性。值得注意的是，N-乙酰谷氨酰谷氨酰胺酰胺（NAGGN）生物合成基因簇（BGCs）仅存在于来自多核藻类目的MAGs中，而多糖利用位点（PULs）存在于所有分析的MAGs中。这些结果表明，与多核藻类相关的细菌群落是复杂的，具有适应挑战性和多变环境的多样化生存策略。

## Abstract
Coenocytic algae are organisms that undergo karyokinesis without cytokinesis, resulting in multinucleated cells. Most research on bacterial communities in coenocytic algae has used 16S rRNA sequencing, primarily focusing on the order Bryopsidales of green coenocytic algae. Recent studies have analyzed metagenome-assembled genomes (MAGs) from algal hosts across multiple taxa, such as Chlorophyta, Phaeophyta, and Rhodophyta, revealing more about bacterial biosynthetic machinery and potential symbiotic relationships. Given the cosmopolitan distribution of coenocytic algae, such as Bryopsis, Caulerpa, Codium, and the yellow-green alga Vaucheria, and their unique morphology, there is a need to better understand their associated bacterial communities. To address this, filaments of Vaucheria bursata LB2067 were sequenced using the Illumina NovaSeq instrument, and all publicly available short- and long-read datasets from coenocytic algae were screened for MAG recovery. All recovered MAGs from both Bryopsidales and Vaucheriales showed a high dominance of Pseudomonadota at the phylum level, but no consistent patterns at the order or family levels. High completeness of specific KEGG pathways, such as bidirectional polyphosphate metabolism and riboflavin biosynthesis, was prevalent in MAGs from coenocytic orders compared to non-coenocytic ones. Notably, N-acetylglutaminylglutamine amide (NAGGN) biosynthetic gene clusters (BGCs) were found only in MAGs from coenocytic orders, whereas polysaccharide utilization loci (PULs) were present in all MAGs analyzed. These results indicate that bacterial communities associated with coenocytic algae are complex, with diverse survival strategies adapted to challenging and variable environments.
