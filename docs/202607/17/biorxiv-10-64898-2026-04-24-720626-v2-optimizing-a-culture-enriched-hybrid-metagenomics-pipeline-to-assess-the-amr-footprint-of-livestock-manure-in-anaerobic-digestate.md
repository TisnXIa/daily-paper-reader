## 摘要
厌氧消化液作为抗微生物耐药性基因（ARGs）库的程度可能被低估，因为常规宏基因组学可能低估低丰度决定因子，并且缺乏足够的分辨率来可靠地将ARGs与可移动遗传元件（MGE）联系起来。本研究使用杂合组装评估了富集培养宏基因组学（CEMG）在有和无抗菌选择性条件下是否改善消化液中ARGs的检测以及ARG-MGE-宿主连接的鉴定。富集培养显著提高了ARG的恢复：平均ARG信号从新鲜消化液（FD；直接宏基因组样本）中的15.4 CPM增加到无抗生素CEMG中的124 CPM和抗生素选择性CEMG中的160.0 CPM，相当于比FD增加了约10.4倍。虽然在FD中仅检测到9个独特的ARGs，但富集培养恢复了112个，包括具有临床重要性的ARGs，如编码万古霉素耐药性、超广谱β-内酰胺酶和利奈唑胺耐药性的基因。氧气可用性成为构建富集培养的最强因素，需氧和厌氧样本形成不同的聚类，并表现出优势类群和耐药组组成的转变。抗生素选择产生了更有针对性的、类特异性转变，其中四环素耐药性在所有处理中一致富集。杂合宏基因组组装进一步解析了ARG-MGE-宿主连接，揭示了ARGs与MGEs和重金属抗性基因的广泛共定位。这些发现共同表明，通过改善低丰度ARGs的检测，抗生素选择性富集培养增强了耐药组监测，而杂合组装提供了评估其移动性和宿主关联的关键基因组背景。

## Abstract
The extent that anaerobic digestate acts as a reservoir of antimicrobial resistance genes (ARGs) is likely underestimated as conventional metagenomics may underrepresent low-abundance determinants and lacks sufficient resolution to reliably link ARGs to mobile genetic elements (MGE). This study used hybrid assemblies to evaluate whether culture-enriched metagenomics (CEMG), with and without antimicrobial selectivity, improves detection of ARGs in digestate and characterization of ARG- MGE-host linkages. Culture enrichment substantially increased ARG recovery: mean ARG signal rose from 15.4 CPM in fresh digestate (FD; direct metagenomic samples) to 124 CPM in CEMG without antibiotics and 160.0 CPM in antibiotic-selective CEMG, corresponding to an approximately 10.4-fold increase over FD. While only 9 unique ARGs were detected in FD, enrichment recovered 112, including those of clinical importance such as those encoding for vancomycin resistance, extended-spectrum {beta}-lactamase, and linezolid resistance. Oxygen availability emerged as the strongest factor structuring enrichment, with aerobic and anaerobic samples forming distinct clusters and exhibiting shifts in dominant taxa and resistome composition. Antibiotic selection produced more targeted, class-specific shifts, with tetracycline resistance consistently enriched across treatments. Hybrid metagenomic assembly further resolved ARG-MGE-host linkages, revealing extensive co-localization of ARGs with MGEs and heavy metal resistance genes. Together, these findings demonstrate that antibiotic-selective culture enrichment enhances resistome surveillance by improving detection of low-abundance ARGs, while hybrid assembly provides critical genomic context to assess their mobility and host associations.
