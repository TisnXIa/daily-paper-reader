## 摘要
背景：叶蝉是全球最重要的植物病原体昆虫媒介之一，它们依赖微生物共生体来利用营养贫乏的韧皮部汁液。然而，大多数关于叶蝉相关微生物群的研究都集中在有限的分类单元或标记基因调查上，导致对这些微生物群落的基因组多样性、生态组织和功能潜力了解不足。在此，我们通过整合来自11个亚科、13个国家、171种叶蝉的基因组解析宏基因组学数据，生成了全球叶蝉微生物组目录，其中包括首次从北极叶蝉中表征的微生物组。

结果：从头组装和基因组重建产生了337个高质量非冗余微生物基因组和1860万个非冗余基因，极大地扩展了已知与叶蝉科相关的微生物多样性，包括几个此前未描述的细菌谱系。比较分析揭示了一种重复出现的模块化微生物组结构，由以下部分组成：(i) 由Candidatus Karelsulcia和Candidatus Nasuia主导的保守核心专性营养共生体；(ii) 包括Wolbachia、Arsenophonus、Rickettsia和Diplorickettsia在内的异质性次级共生体层；以及(iii) 一个动态的环境获得性细菌库。尽管专性共生体在不同宿主中高度保守，但次级和环境分类单元在物种和地区之间差异显著，表明重复的获得是由生态过滤而非仅宿主系统发育塑造的。对专食性玉米叶蝉Dalbulus maidis和更广食性的紫菀叶蝉Macrosteles quadrilineatus的比较分析进一步表明，近缘媒介可以保持保守的祖先共生体，同时拥有显著不同的附属微生物组。北极种群含有独特的微生物组合，富含与耐寒性、氧化应激和生殖操控相关的功能。此外，我们鉴定出多种植物相关细菌，包括植原体、螺原体、泛菌属和欧文氏菌属，以及具有预测营养和促植物生长功能的分类单元。

结论：我们的发现揭示叶蝉微生物组是通过古老的专性共生和灵活的环境响应微生物层相互作用而构建的。这项工作建立了一个基因组解析框架，用于理解昆虫媒介中的微生物组进化，并强调了微生物群落结构在宿主适应、病原体生态和可持续害虫管理中的潜在作用。

## Abstract
BackgroundLeafhoppers are among the most important insect vectors of plant pathogens worldwide and depend on microbial symbionts to exploit nutrient-poor phloem diets. However, most studies of leafhopper-associated microbiota have focused on a limited number of taxa or marker-gene surveys, leaving the genomic diversity, ecological organization, and functional potential of these microbial communities poorly understood. Here, we generated the Global Leafhopper Microbiome Catalog by integrating genome-resolved metagenomics from 171 leafhopper species across 11 subfamilies and 13 countries, including the first microbiomes characterized from Arctic leafhoppers.

ResultsDe novo assembly and genome reconstruction generated 337 high-quality non-redundant microbial genomes and 18.6 million non-redundant genes, substantially expanding the known microbial diversity associated with Cicadellidae, including several previously undescribed bacterial lineages. Comparative analyses revealed a recurrent modular microbiome architecture composed of: (i) a conserved core of obligate nutritional symbionts, dominated by  Candidatus Karelsulcia and  Candidatus Nasuia; (ii) a heterogeneous layer of secondary symbionts, including Wolbachia, Arsenophonus, Rickettsia, and Diplorickettsia; and (iii) a dynamic pool of environmentally acquired bacteria. While obligate symbionts remained highly conserved across divergent hosts, secondary and environmental taxa varied substantially among species and regions, suggesting repeated acquisition shaped by ecological filtering rather than host phylogeny alone. Comparative analyses between the specialist corn leafhopper Dalbulus maidis and the more polyphagous aster leafhopper Macrosteles quadrilineatus further showed that closely related vectors can maintain conserved ancestral symbionts while harboring markedly distinct accessory microbiomes. Arctic populations contained unique microbial assemblages enriched in functions associated with cold tolerance, oxidative stress, and reproductive manipulation. In addition, we identified numerous plant-associated bacteria, including phytoplasmas, spiroplasmas, Pantoea, and Erwinia, alongside taxa with predicted nutritional and plant growth-promoting functions.

ConclusionsOur findings reveal that leafhopper microbiomes are structured through the interaction of ancient obligate symbioses and flexible environmentally responsive microbial layers. This work establishes a genome-resolved framework for understanding microbiome evolution in insect vectors and highlights the potential role of microbial community structure in host adaptation, pathogen ecology, and sustainable pest management.
