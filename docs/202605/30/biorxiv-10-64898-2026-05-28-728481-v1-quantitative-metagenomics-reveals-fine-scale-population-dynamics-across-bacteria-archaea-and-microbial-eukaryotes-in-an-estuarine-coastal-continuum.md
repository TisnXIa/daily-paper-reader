## 摘要
微生物群落是海洋生态系统功能的基础，然而其多样性常因宽泛的分类分组和相对丰度调查而被掩盖，这些方法无法揭示单个种群的动态。这一限制在河口-海岸梯度中尤为重要，因为微生物现存量、环境条件和群落组成在空间和时间上剧烈变化。本研究采用定量、基因组解析的宏基因组学方法，对横跨纽斯河河口、帕姆利科湾及邻近北大西洋陆架水域的一年期河口-海洋断面进行微生物种群动态分析。通过内标归一化，实现了单拷贝基因和宏基因组组装基因组（MAGs）的绝对丰度估算，从而能够以每升基因组当量追踪单个种群。河口水域的细菌现存量较高，群落主要随盐度和季节变化。我们回收了415个MAGs，包括386个细菌基因组（平均占细菌基因组当量的52%），以及古菌和真核生物代表。许多高丰度的MAGs缺乏近缘参考基因组，表明数量上重要的海岸种群仍未得到充分表征。基因组解析的丰度揭示了密切相关类群间的显著生态位分化，包括与不同色素细胞计量群相关的聚球菌属、蓝细菌属和Vulcanococcus种群的季节性和空间演替。红杆菌科MAGs还与微微型真核生物MAGs显示出种群特异的关联，例如在冬季外海与Micromonas相关水华期间，一种Planktomarina种群达到总细菌基因组当量的18%。通过提供绝对种群丰度，本研究将海岸微生物组调查转化为数值框架，用于解析动态环境梯度中的微生物种群结构、生态相互作用和生物地球化学相关性。

## Abstract
Microbial communities are foundational to marine ecosystem function, yet their diversity is often obscured by broad taxonomic groupings and relative-abundance surveys that mask the dynamics of individual populations. This limitation is especially important across estuarine-coastal gradients, where microbial standing stocks, environmental conditions, and community composition vary sharply over space and time. Here, we used quantitative, genome-resolved metagenomics to examine microbial population dynamics across a one-year estuary-to-ocean transect spanning the Neuse River Estuary, Pamlico Sound, and adjacent North Atlantic shelf waters. Internal standard normalization enabled absolute abundance estimates for single-copy genes and metagenome-assembled genomes (MAGs), allowing individual populations to be tracked as genome equivalents per liter. Bacterial standing stocks were higher in estuarine waters, and communities varied primarily with salinity and season. We recovered 415 MAGs, including 386 bacterial genomes that represented, on average, 52% of bacterial genome equivalents, along with archaeal and eukaryotic representatives. Many abundant MAGs lacked close reference genomes, demonstrating that numerically important coastal populations remain poorly characterized. Genome-resolved abundances revealed pronounced niche partitioning among closely related taxa, including seasonal and spatial turnover of Synechococcus, Cyanobium, and Vulcanococcus populations associated with distinct pigment-defined cytometric groups. Rhodobacteraceae MAGs also showed population-specific correlations with picoeukaryotic MAGs, including a winter offshore Planktomarina population that reached 18% of total bacterial genome equivalents during a Micromonas-associated bloom. By providing absolute population abundances, this study transformed coastal microbiome surveys into numerical frameworks for resolving microbial population structure, ecological interactions, and biogeochemical relevance across dynamic environmental gradients.
