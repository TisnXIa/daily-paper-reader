## 摘要
长读长扩增子测序可以比短读长测序获得更长的序列，但恢复扩增子序列变异(ASVs)对于长且噪音高的读长来说是一个挑战。我们提出了savont，一种从现代长读长扩增子中恢复ASVs的算法，其平均准确率≥98%，包括Oxford Nanopore Technologies (ONT) R10.4和PacBio HiFi读取。对于全长16S rRNA ONT读取，savont所需的测序深度比以往方法低5到16倍，并且为复杂环境生成的ASVs数量最多可达95倍。savont使得基于浅层且噪声高的长读长扩增子测序的ASV分析变得可行。

## Abstract
Long-read amplicon sequencing can profile longer sequences compared to short reads, but recovering amplicon sequence variants (ASVs) is a challenge for long, noisy reads. We present savont, an algorithm for recovering ASVs from modern long-read amplicons with mean accuracy [&ge;] 98%, including Oxford Nanopore Technologies (ONT) R10.4 and PacBio HiFi reads. Savont requires 5 to 16 times lower sequencing depth for full-length 16S rRNA ONT reads than previous methods and generates up to 95 times more ASVs for complex environments. Savont makes ASV-based analysis from shallow and noisy long-read amplicon sequencing feasible.
