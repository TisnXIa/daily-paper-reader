## 摘要
类Polinton病毒（PLVs）是水环境中最为丰富的真核DNA病毒之一。尽管它们具有广泛的多样性、广泛的宿主范围和可变基因内容，但通常被视为单一类群，这模糊了它们的进化关系并使其分类复杂化。通过分析来自水生生态系统和公共宏基因组数据集的数千个病毒基因组，我们澄清了“PLV”这一术语所涵盖的进化结构。利用灵敏的谱隐马尔可夫模型（HMM）比较、保守衣壳形态发生基因的系统发育以及基因含量分析，我们表明被称为PLV的病毒分布在至少跨越三个当前公认的病毒类的多个深部分支中。这些包括Gosseviruses，即与动物基因组中的Maverick-Polintons相关的水生病毒。它们还包括从15 kb PLV到45 kb Mriyaviruses乃至更广泛的核质病毒门的一系列相关病毒，可能代表了巨型病毒的现存近亲。我们的发现表明，PLVs并不完全符合现有的分类界限，反映了水平基因转移和生命策略多样化的复杂历史。为支持未来的发现，我们提供了一个精选的HMM集合，代表已知的PLVs、Maverick-Polintons和病毒噬菌体的衣壳多样性。该工具包能够灵敏地检测和识别宏基因组和真核基因组数据集中的PLVs。我们的研究为解释PLV多样性提供了一个进化框架，并为其分类的未来完善奠定了基础。

## Abstract
Polinton-like viruses (PLVs) are among the most abundant eukaryotic DNA viruses in aquatic environments. Despite their extensive diversity, broad host range and variable gene content, they are commonly treated as a single group, which obscures their evolutionary relationships and complicates their classification. Through analysing thousands of viral genomes from aquatic ecosystems and public metagenomic datasets, we clarify the evolutionary structure encompassed by the term PLV. Using sensitive profile Hidden Markov Model (HMM) comparisons, phylogenies of conserved capsid morphogenetic genes and gene content analysis, we show that viruses referred to as PLVs are distributed across multiple deep lineages spanning at least three currently recognised viral classes. These include the Gosseviruses, aquatic viruses related to Maverick-Polintons in animal genomes. They also include a continuum of related viruses from 15 kb PLVs to the 45 kb Mriyaviruses and more broadly, to the Nucleocytoviricota, potentially representing extant relatives of giant viruses. Our findings suggest that PLVs do not fit neatly within existing taxonomic boundaries, reflecting a complex history of horizontal gene transfer and diversification of life strategies. To support future discovery, we provide a curated set of HMMs representing the known capsid diversity of PLVs, Maverick-Polintons, and virophages. This toolkit enables sensitive detection and identification of PLVs across metagenomic and eukaryotic genome datasets. Our study provides an evolutionary framework for interpreting PLV diversity and a foundation for future refinement of their classification.
