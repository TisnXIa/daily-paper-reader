## 摘要
非消化性低聚糖被广泛用作益生元，但结构相关的聚糖可能引发不同的肠道微生物组响应。这里，我们结合了受控离体发酵、深度DIA宏蛋白质组学和靶向代谢组学，以确定低聚糖结构和供体年龄如何塑造微生物组功能。来自三个年龄组的18名健康供体的粪便微生物组与来自两个聚糖家族——果寡糖（FOS）和半乳糖基蔗糖衍生物（GSD）的七种结构相关低聚糖共同培养。

我们发现低聚糖结构组织了一个功能响应景观，而不仅仅是简单地将底物分为宽泛的益生元类别。结构相关的聚糖总体上产生了更相似的响应谱，但密切相关的FOS底物在功能上仍然可区分，表明微生物组将细微的结构差异解析为分级的功能变化。这些结构响应性功能进一步与相对于基线的生产者层次重组相关，而靶向酶水平分析表明，底物特异性CAZyme响应也可能反映共享生产者背景下功能投入的改变。尽管存在这些底物特异性进入过程，网络分析揭示了向共享下游生理状态的汇聚，这些状态富含翻译、氨基酸生物合成、分泌/输出和趋化性相关通路。在所有处理中，主要短链脂肪酸增加，而黏蛋白聚糖降解相关标志物减少，表明向糖酵解代谢协调转变并减少宿主聚糖觅食。色氨酸相关代谢也与初级果聚糖加工一致相关，伴随着更高的细胞外色氨酸可用性。

供体年龄改变了选定的微生物功能轴和酶-代谢物耦合关系，而不是核心发酵产物的总体方向。特别是，低聚糖减弱了老年人微生物组中史氏甲烷短杆菌（M. smithii）和M00567产甲烷相关特征，并改变了丁酸途径酶与细胞外丁酸水平之间的年龄依赖性关系。

总之，这些发现表明低聚糖结构决定了肠道微生物群落如何组织碳水化合物加工和下游功能状态，而供体年龄重塑了这些响应的分类学和代谢背景。这项工作为结构意识和年龄意识的精准益生元设计提供了机制框架。

## Abstract
Non-digestible oligosaccharides are widely used as prebiotics, yet structurally related glycans can elicit distinct gut microbiome responses. Here, we combined controlled ex vivo fermentation, deep DIA metaproteomics, and targeted metabolomics to determine how oligosaccharide structure and donor age shape microbiome function. Stool microbiomes from 18 healthy donors across three age groups were cultured with seven structurally related oligosaccharides from two glycan families, fructo-oligosaccharides (FOS) and galactosyl-sucrose derivatives (GSD).

We found that oligosaccharide structure organized a functional response landscape rather than simply separating substrates into broad prebiotic classes. Structurally related glycans produced more similar response profiles overall, yet closely related FOS substrates remained functionally distinguishable, indicating that subtle structural differences were resolved by the microbiome as graded functional changes. These structure-responsive functions were further associated with producer-level reorganization relative to baseline, while targeted enzyme-level analyses indicated that substrate-specific CAZyme responses could also reflect altered functional investment within shared producer backgrounds. Despite these substrate-specific entry processes, network analysis revealed convergence onto shared downstream physiological states enriched for translation, amino-acid biosynthesis, secretion/export, and chemotaxis-related pathways. Across treatments, major short-chain fatty acids increased while mucin glycan degradation-associated markers decreased, suggesting coordinated shifts toward saccharolytic metabolism and reduced host-glycan foraging. Tryptophan-associated metabolism was also consistently linked to primary fructan processing, accompanied by higher extracellular tryptophan availability.

Donor age modified selected microbial functional axes and enzyme-metabolite coupling relationships rather than the overall direction of core fermentation outputs. In particular, oligosaccharides attenuated an Methanobrevibacter smithii (M. smithii) and M00567 methanogenesis-related signature in microbiomes from older adults and altered age-dependent relationships between butyrate-pathway enzymes and extracellular butyrate levels.

Together, these findings show that oligosaccharide structure determines how gut microbial communities organize carbohydrate processing and downstream functional states, while donor age reshapes the taxonomic and metabolic context of these responses. This work provides a mechanistic framework for structure-aware and age-aware precision prebiotic design.
