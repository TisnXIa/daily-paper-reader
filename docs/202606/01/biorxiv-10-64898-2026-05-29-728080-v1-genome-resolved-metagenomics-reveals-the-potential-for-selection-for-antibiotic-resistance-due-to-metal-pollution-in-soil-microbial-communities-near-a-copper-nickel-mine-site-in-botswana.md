## 摘要
在全球南方国家，管理不善和金属污染问题严重，常常为土壤微生物群落创造具有挑战性的极端条件。然而，撒哈拉以南非洲污染地区的土壤很少被采样。因此，这些地区是否存在抗生素抗性传播的潜力仍不清楚。在本研究中，我们对受博茨瓦纳铜镍矿影响的区域周围梯度样本进行了基因组分辨宏基因组学分析，以确定矿区污染对抗生素抗性共选择的潜力。具体而言，我们在博茨瓦纳矿区梯度上采样并进行基因组分辨宏基因组学分析。总体上，污染程度随靠近矿区而增加，而原核生物多样性随距矿区距离增加而增加，并且我们识别出少数与金属浓度显著相关的分类群。此外，我们组装的contig表明，由于这些基因兼具抗生素抗性基因（ARGs）和金属抗性基因（MRGs）的双重功能（作为多药外排泵），存在共选择的潜力。相比之下，一些ARGs与MRGs在同一区域共存，表明由于共定位而存在共选择的潜力。然而，在contig水平上，我们未检测到ARGs/MRGs与水平基因转移（HGT）标记的任何共定位。我们将contig分箱为MAGs，发现数个高质量且完整的MAGs（属于放线菌门、绿弯菌门、酸杆菌门和休眠杆菌门等分类群）携带ARGs、MRGs和HGT标记。此外，分箱后我们在八个MAGs中发现了ARGs、MRGs和HGT标记，表明HGT标记与ARG和MRG的出现无直接关联。总之，我们的宏基因组分析表明，在撒哈拉以南非洲等采样不足的地区，矿区污染可导致AMR（特别是具有广谱底物如外排泵且可作为MRGs的ARGs）的共选择。

## Abstract
Mismanagement and metal pollution are high in countries in the global south and often create challenging, extreme conditions for soil microbial communities. However, soils from polluted sites in Sub-Saharan areas are rarely sampled. Thus, it remains unclear whether the potential for dissemination of antibiotic resistance exists in these areas. In the present study, we performed genome-resolved metagenomics on samples across a gradient around an area impacted by a copper-nickel mine in Botswana to identify the potential for co-selection for antibiotic resistance due to mine pollution. Specifically, we sampled over a gradient across a mine area in Botswana and performed genome-resolved metagenomics. In general, pollution increased with proximity to the mine, while prokaryotic diversity increased with increasing distance from the mine, and we could identify a few taxa that significantly correlated with the metal concentrations. Moreover, our assembled contigs indicate a potential for co-selection due to the dual function of these genes as both antibiotic resistance genes (ARGs) and metal resistance genes (MRGs), as they act as multidrug efflux pumps. In contrast, some ARGs co-occurred in the same region as MRGs, indicating the potential for co-selection due to co-localization. However, we could not detect any co-localization of ARGs/MRGs with horizontal gene transfer (HGT) markers at the contig level. We binned the contigs to MAGs, and we found several MAGs of high quality and completeness, belonging to taxa such as Actinobacteriota, Chloroflexota, Acidobacteriota, and Dormibacterota, that possess ARGs, MRGs, and HGT-markers. Moreover, we found ARGs, MRGs, and HGT-markers in eight MAGs, after binning, indicating no direct association of HGT-markers with ARG and MRG occurrence. In summary, our metagenomic analysis indicates that pollution in mines can lead to co-selection of AMR, specifically ARGs with a broad spectrum of substrates (e.g., efflux pumps) that can act as MRGs, in an under-sampled location such as Sub-Saharan countries.
