## 摘要
气候变暖加速了冰川融水向北极湖泊的输送，从融化的流域中 mobilized 金属，重塑了当地微生物所经历的选择景观。这些梯度是否在环境抗性基因中留下可检测的进化特征尚不清楚。我们研究了来自加拿大努纳武特地区黑曾湖——高北极最大的淡水湖——的宏基因组数据集中的四个金属抗性基因（merA、arsC、cadA 和 chrR），这些样本采集自一个包含对照、低径流和高径流状态的自然水文梯度。采用空间换时间设计，我们结合了群体遗传学和基于密码子的方法来量化多样性和选择，包括核苷酸多样性、Tajima's D、非同义/同义多样性比、McDonald-Kreitman检验、位点水平上的间歇性选择，以及基因库多样性和交换的贝叶斯溯祖和结构化溯祖推断。这种多管齐下的方法使我们能够区分基因特异性的进化响应和更广泛的人口统计学效应。我们发现基因之间存在显著异质性：merA 沿径流梯度表现出增加的一致适应性进化证据；cadA 在低径流状态下显示出最强的适应性信号，且选择约束模式在不同状态下变化；chrR 表现出最明显的间歇性正选择信号，其基因范围选择和适应性替换集中在铬浓度最高的高径流状态；而 arsC 在不同状态下基本与中性进化一致。总之，这些结果表明，气候驱动的金属动员与北极土壤微生物组中微生物金属抗性基因库的基因特异性和状态特异性多样化相关。金属形态的差异（砷和铬以氧化还原敏感性含氧阴离子形式存在，而汞、镉和锌以二价阳离子形式存在）可能有助于观察到的跨基因对比进化轨迹，尽管该数据集未评估原位形态。我们的研究结果强调了环境抗性基因作为快速变暖的极地生态系统中生物地球化学条件变化的敏感指标。

## Abstract
Climate warming accelerates glacial meltwater delivery to Arctic lakes, mobilizing metals from thawing catchments and reshaping the selective landscape experienced by resident microbes. Whether these gradients leave detectable evolutionary signatures in environmental resistance genes remains unclear. We investigated four metal resistance genes (merA, arsC, cadA, and chrR) in metagenomic datasets from Lake Hazen (Nunavut, Canada), the largest High Arctic freshwater lake, sampled across a natural hydrological gradient of Control, Low-runoff, and High-runoff regimes. Using a space-for-time design, we combined population-genetic and codon-based approaches to quantify diversity and selection, including nucleotide diversity, Tajimas D, nonsynonymous/synonymous diversity ratios, McDonald-Kreitman tests, site-level episodic selection, and Bayesian coalescent and structured-coalescent inference of gene-pool diversity and exchange. This multi-pronged approach allowed us to distinguish gene-specific evolutionary responses from broader demographic effects. We found marked heterogeneity among genes: merA showed increasing diversity and consistent evidence of adaptive evolution along the runoff gradient; cadA displayed the strongest adaptive signal under low runoff, with selective constraint patterns that varied across regimes; chrR exhibited the clearest signature of episodic positive selection, with gene-wide selection and adaptive substitutions concentrated in the high-runoff regime where chromium concentrations were greatest; while arsC remained largely consistent with neutral evolution across regimes. Together, these results show that climate-driven metal mobilization is associated with gene- and regime-specific diversification of microbial metal resistance gene pools in Arctic soil microbiomes. Differences in metal speciation (with arsenic and chromium occurring as redox-sensitive oxyanions, and mercury, cadmium and zinc as divalent cations) may contribute to the contrasting evolutionary trajectories observed across genes, although in-situ speciation was not assessed in this dataset. Our findings highlight environmental resistance genes as sensitive indicators of changing biogeochemical conditions in rapidly warming polar ecosystems.
