## 摘要
坏死性肠炎（NE）由产气荚膜梭菌引起，是家禽中一种重要的肠道疾病，具有显著的经济影响。NE常因与艾美耳球虫共感染而触发，但艾美耳球和产气荚膜梭菌在NE诱导的菌群失调和疾病进展中的相对作用仍不清楚。为此，用最大艾美耳球虫、产气荚膜梭菌或两者同时攻毒Cobb肉鸡，并利用16S rRNA基因测序和鸟枪法宏基因组学分析回肠和盲肠微生物群。进一步在感染后多个时间点评估肠道微生物群变化的时间动态。我们的研究结果表明，NE相关菌群失调的特征是包括产气荚膜梭菌、埃希氏菌和鸡肠球菌在内的条件致病菌富集，以及某些产乳酸和短链脂肪酸的细菌的差异丰度。菌群失调和疾病进展由最大艾美耳球虫和产气荚膜梭菌之间的协同相互作用驱动，其中最大艾美耳球虫发挥主导影响。值得注意的是，即使没有预先暴露，单独的最大艾美耳球虫也能促进共生产气荚膜梭菌或密切相关细菌的扩增。对盲肠微生物群的宏基因组分析进一步揭示了功能转变，即响应最大艾美耳球虫和NE攻毒，倾向于利用宿主来源的聚糖和简单碳水化合物而非膳食纤维。这种转变与最大艾美耳球虫诱导的上皮损伤、黏液生成增加和营养吸收不良同时发生。NE相关菌群失调在最大艾美耳球虫感染后四天出现，并在产气荚膜梭菌攻毒后2-3天达到高峰。这些发现表明，艾美耳球虫感染为产气荚膜梭菌的定植和增殖创造了允许性肠道环境，强调了其在NE发病机制中的关键作用。

重要性：NE是家禽生产中的主要健康和经济负担，主要由产气荚膜梭菌驱动，并由艾美耳球虫感染增强。本研究为最大艾美耳球虫和产气荚膜梭菌在NE疾病进展中的独特和协同作用提供了关键的机制性见解。我们表明，艾美耳球虫通过诱导上皮损伤、炎症和营养吸收不良，在驱动NE相关菌群失调和疾病进展中起主导作用，从而促进产气荚膜梭菌的定植、增殖和毒素产生。对微生物组结构和功能变化的全面表征揭示，NE相关菌群失调的标志是兼性条件致病菌的富集，这些细菌倾向于利用宿主来源的粘蛋白和简单的膳食碳水化合物，同时伴随严格厌氧、纤维发酵、产短链脂肪酸细菌的消耗。这些微生物变化反映了疾病进展，并为早期诊断和治疗干预提供了潜在的生物标志物。我们的发现为基于微生物群的诊断和干预措施奠定了基础，以减轻NE和其他肠道疾病。

## Abstract
Necrotic enteritis (NE), caused by Clostridium perfringens, is a major enteric disease in poultry with substantial economic impact. NE is frequently triggered by co-infection with Eimeria spp., yet the relative contributions of Eimeria and C. perfringens to NE-induced dysbiosis and disease progression remain poorly defined. To address this, Cobb broiler chickens were challenged with Eimeria maxima, C. perfringens, or both, and ileal and cecal microbiota were analyzed using 16S rRNA gene sequencing and shotgun metagenomics. Temporal dynamics of intestinal microbiota shifts were further assessed at multiple time points post-infection. Our findings demonstrate that NE-associated dysbiosis is characterized by enrichment of pathobionts including C. perfringens, Escherichia, and Enterococcus cecorum, along with differential abundance of certain lactic acid-and short chain fatty acid-producing bacteria. Dysbiosis and disease progression were driven by synergistic interactions between E. maxima and C. perfringens, with E. maxima exerting a dominant influence. Notably, E. maxima alone promoted expansion of commensal C. perfringens or closely-related bacteria, even without prior exposure. Metagenomic analysis of the cecal microbiota further revealed a functional shift favoring utilization of host-derived glycans and simple carbohydrates over dietary fibers, in response to E. maxima and NE challenges. This transition coincided with E. maxima-induced epithelial damage, increased mucogenesis, and nutrient malabsorption. NE-associated dysbiosis emerged four days post-E. maxima infection and peaked 2-3 days following C. perfringens challenge. These findings suggest that Eimeria infection creates a permissive intestinal environment for C. perfringens colonization and proliferation, underscoring its pivotal role in NE pathogenesis.

IMPORTANCENE is a major health and economic burden in poultry production, primarily driven by C. perfringens and potentiated by Eimeria infection. This study provides critical mechanistic insights into the distinct and synergistic roles of E. maxima and C. perfringens in NE disease progression. We show that Eimeria plays a dominant role in driving NE-associated dysbiosis and disease progression by inducing epithelial damage, inflammation, and nutrient malabsorption, which facilitate C. perfringens colonization, proliferation, and toxin production. Comprehensive characterizations of both structural and functional microbiome shifts revealed that NE-associated dysbiosis is marked by enrichment of facultative pathobionts that favors utilization of host-derived mucins and simple dietary carbohydrates, alongside depletion of strictly anaerobic, fiber-fermenting, SCFA-producing bacteria. These microbial shifts reflect disease progression and offer potential biomarkers for early diagnosis and therapeutic intervention. Our findings lay a foundation for microbiota-based diagnostics and interventions to mitigate NE and other enteric diseases.
