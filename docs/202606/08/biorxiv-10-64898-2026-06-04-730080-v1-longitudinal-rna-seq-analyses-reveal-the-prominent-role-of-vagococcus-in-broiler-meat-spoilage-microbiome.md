## 摘要
背景 生鸡肉产品极易腐败，微生物活动是限制其货架期的主要因素。鸡肉产品腐败微生物组的研究主要依赖传统培养方法和16S rRNA基因扩增测序，但这些方法均无法展示整个微生物组的活性。此前关于鸡肉腐败的宏转录组研究也仅限于特定腐败菌，而非整个微生物组。结果 我们对鸡肉腐败的纵向研究揭示了在4°C和6°C条件下活性细菌微生物组的演替以及腐败菌的代谢通路活性。每日采集样品进行宏转录组分析，并结合非靶向代谢组学、传统微生物学和感官分析，直至发生深度腐败。在两种温度下，Carnobacterium divergens、Carnobacterium maltaromaticum和Vagococcus proximus是最活跃的物种。已知卡诺杆菌是家禽腐败菌，而最近描述的Vagococcus proximus则在微生物组中扮演了意想不到的活跃角色。在6°C储存的样品中，它成为优势菌，并且在保质期后其活性在4°C下也有所增加。中心碳水化合物代谢是两种温度下微生物组最常见的KEGG直系同源通路模块。C. divergens和C. maltaromaticum在腐败过程中表现出稳定的代谢特征，而V. proximus在4°C储存样品中腐败加深时，表现出从高ATP合成活性向脂肪酸和碳水化合物代谢增强的转变。非靶向代谢组学显示两种温度下代谢组趋势相似。在6°C下，时间依赖性变化通常更为显著，腐败标志物酪胺和亚精胺积累更多。结论 正如预期，6°C下的腐败速率高于4°C，但我们未预料到腐败过程的整体轨迹如此相似。我们的结果将V. proximus与鸡肉中的关键活性腐败菌联系起来，并证明了RNA-seq与代谢组学结合用于解析肉类腐败微生物组功能的有效性。这表明腐败微生物组包含一些因标准方法的技术限制而被我们忽视的活性物种。未来有必要针对Vagococcus的代谢和检测开展研究。

## Abstract
Background Raw broiler meat products are highly perishable, and microbial activity is the main factor limiting their shelf lives. The spoilage microbiomes of broiler meat products have been studied mainly using traditional culturing methods and 16S rRNA gene amplicon sequencing, neither of which can show the activity of whole microbiome. Previous metatranscriptomic research of broiler spoilage has also remained limited to specific spoilage organisms rather than entire microbiomes. Results Our longitudinal study of broiler meat spoilage discovered the successions of active bacterial microbiomes and metabolic pathway activities of spoilers at 4C and 6C. Samples taken daily were subjected to metatranscriptomic analyses in combination with non-targeted metabolomic, traditional microbiology, and sensory analyses until advanced spoilage took place. Carnobacterium divergens, Carnobacterium maltaromaticum and Vagococcus proximus were the most active species at both temperatures. Carnobacteria are known poultry spoilers whereas Vagococcus proximus, a species recently described, played an unexpected active role in the microbiome. It became dominant in samples stored at 6C and its activity increased also at 4C after the use-by date. Central carbohydrate metabolism was the most common KEGG orthology pathway module of the microbiome at both temperatures. C. divergens and C. maltaromaticum showed stable metabolic profiles during the spoilage process, whereas V. proximus displayed a shift from high ATP synthesis activity to increased fatty acid and carbohydrate metabolism when spoilage advanced in samples stored at 4C. Non-targeted metabolomics showed similar metabolomic trends across both temperatures. At 6C, time-dependent changes were generally more pronounced, and the spoilage markers tyramine and spermidine showed greater accumulation. Conclusions As expected, the rate of spoilage is higher at 6 than 4C, however we did not anticipate a similar overall trajectory of the spoilage processes. Our results link V. proximus as a key active spoiler in broiler meat and demonstrate the efficacy of using RNA-seq together with metabolomics to decode the function of a meat spoilage microbiome. This demonstrates that spoilage microbiomes consist of active species we have been neglecting due to the technological limitations of the standard methods. Future studies targeting to the metabolism and detecting of Vagococcus are warranted.
