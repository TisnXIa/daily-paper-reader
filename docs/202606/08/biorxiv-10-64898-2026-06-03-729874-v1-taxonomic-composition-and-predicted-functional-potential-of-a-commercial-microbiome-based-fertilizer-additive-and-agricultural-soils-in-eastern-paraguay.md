## 摘要
人为土壤退化是可持续粮食生产的主要挑战，特别是在热带农业系统中，过量施肥会导致土壤退化和温室气体排放。基于微生物组的农业技术为提高肥料利用效率同时保持作物生产力提供了一种潜在策略。本研究对巴拉圭和乌拉圭超过140万公顷土地上使用的商业微生物肥料添加剂（腐殖土）进行了分类组成和预测功能潜力的表征，并将其与根相关微生物组进行了比较。同时，评估了巴拉圭东部的农业和森林土壤微生物组。使用16S rRNA基因测序和基于PICRUSt2的功能预测分析了微生物群落。腐殖土微生物组中与有机化合物降解、养分循环和植物促生活动相关的通路富集。此外，腐殖土和根相关微生物组共享超过350种预测的微生物通路，表明尽管在特定细菌分类群上存在差异，但功能上存在显著重叠，提示该联合体可能像一个根际样微生物群落，能够提供通常与植物相关微生物组相关的功能。在农业土壤中，高产田和低产田之间观察到显著的分类学差异，而预测的功能谱基本保守，这与土壤微生物群落内的功能冗余一致。高产土壤中富集了去甲基甲萘醌-6生物合成II的超通路，这是一种与呼吸代谢相关的微生物维生素K2相关通路。这些发现首次提供了大规模商业微生物肥料添加剂的详细分类和预测功能表征，并为理解巴拉圭高产农业土壤中的微生物多样性和功能潜力建立了基线。

重要性：土壤退化和低效肥料使用是可持续农业的主要制约因素，特别是在养分损失和温室气体排放高的热带系统中。基于微生物组的农业投入品越来越多地被提出作为增强土壤功能和改善养分循环效率的工具，但其生态特征和功能潜力仍知之甚少。本研究首次详细描述了南美农业中使用的大规模商业微生物肥料添加剂的分类和预测功能特征，并将其置于原生森林和农业土壤微生物组的背景下。通过整合16S rRNA基因测序与预测功能分析，本工作揭示了微生物联合体与植物相关微生物组之间的显著功能重叠，表明其向根际样功能的生态趋同。此外，在不同生产力的土壤中识别出保守的功能谱，突显了功能冗余在不同管理模式下维持生态系统过程中的潜在作用。这些发现为理解亚热带农业生态系统（特别是巴拉圭）中基于微生物组的农业投入品和土壤微生物功能稳定性提供了基础框架，有助于发展更可持续的农业实践。

## Abstract
Anthropogenic soil degradation is a major challenge for sustainable food production, particularly in tropical agricultural systems where excessive fertilizer use contributes to soil deterioration and greenhouse gas emissions. Microbiome-based agricultural technologies offer a potential strategy to improve fertilizer-use efficiency while maintaining crop productivity. Here, we characterized the taxonomic composition and predicted functional potential of a commercial microbiome-based fertilizer additive (humus) deployed across more than 1.4 million hectares in Paraguay and Uruguay, and compared it with root-associated microbiomes. In parallel, we evaluated agricultural and forest soil microbiomes from eastern Paraguay. Microbial communities were analyzed using 16S rRNA gene sequencing and PICRUSt2-based functional prediction. The humus microbiome displayed enrichment of pathways associated with degradation of organic compounds, nutrient cycling, and plant-growth-promoting activities. Furthermore, humus and root-associated microbiomes shared over 350 predicted microbial pathways, indicating substantial functional overlap despite differences in specific bacterial taxa, and suggesting that the consortium may function as a rhizosphere-like microbial community capable of providing functions commonly associated with plant-associated microbiomes. In agricultural soils, significant taxonomic differences were observed between high- and low-productivity fields, whereas predicted functional profiles remained largely conserved, consistent with functional redundancy within soil microbial communities. Productive soils were enriched in the superpathway of demethylmenaquinol-6 biosynthesis II, a microbial vitamin K2-related pathway involved in respiratory metabolism. Together, these findings provide the first detailed taxonomic and predicted functional characterization of a large-scale commercial microbiome-based fertilizer additive and establish a baseline for understanding microbial diversity and functional potential across productive agricultural soils in Paraguay.

ImportanceSoil degradation and inefficient fertilizer use are major constraints to sustainable agriculture, particularly in tropical systems where nutrient losses and greenhouse gas emissions are high. Microbiome-based agricultural inputs are increasingly proposed as tools to enhance soil functioning and improve nutrient cycling efficiency, yet their ecological characteristics and functional potential remain poorly understood. This study provides the first detailed taxonomic and predicted functional characterization of a large-scale commercial microbiome-based fertilizer additive deployed in South American agriculture, and places it in the context of native forest and agricultural soil microbiomes.

By integrating 16S rRNA gene sequencing with predictive functional profiling, this work reveals substantial functional overlap between the microbial consortium and plant-associated microbiomes, suggesting ecological convergence toward rhizosphere-like functions. In addition, the identification of conserved functional profiles across soils with contrasting productivity highlights the potential role of functional redundancy in maintaining ecosystem processes under different management regimes. Together, these findings provide a foundational framework for understanding microbiome-based agricultural inputs and soil microbial functional stability in subtropical agroecosystems, specifically Paraguay, contributing to the development of more sustainable agricultural practices.
