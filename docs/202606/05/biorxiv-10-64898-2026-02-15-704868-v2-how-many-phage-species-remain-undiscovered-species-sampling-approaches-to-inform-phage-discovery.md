## 摘要
耐药菌的出现已被确定为近期最严重的公共卫生和发展威胁之一。使用噬菌体是可持续控制这些病原体的一种有前景的解决方案。噬菌体是细菌病原体的天然病毒捕食者。然而，由于细菌的变异性和适应性，开发有效且可持续的噬菌体治疗需要利用多种不同的噬菌体物种。本研究将专门的数学和计算估计方法应用于微生物群落中噬菌体物种的采样和发现问题。我们的目标是基于八种常见细菌宿主在噬菌体序列数据库中记录的物种，预测未来样本中将发现多少新的噬菌体物种。虽然内部验证表明，当前估计器在预测采样更多噬菌体时可发现的新物种数量时误差较低，但这些估计器关键假设是噬菌体采样和数据库纳入的潜在机制随时间保持相似——除了宿主属分枝杆菌外，我们的分析对这一假设提出了质疑。由此，我们讨论了我们的结果如何仍能为未来的采样策略提供信息——既通过直接预测额外物种的数量，也通过检测采样和数据库纳入的变化。我们的结果有潜力为从自然环境中寻找和分离新型噬菌体提供信息并优化这一过程。

## Abstract
The emergence of antimicrobial resistant bacteria has been identified as one of the most serious public health and development threats for the near future. The use of bacteriophages (phages) is a promising solution for the sustainable control of these pathogens. Phages are natural viral predators of bacterial pathogens. However, due to the variability and adaptability of bacteria, developing effective and sustainable phage treatments requires drawing from a wide variety of different phage species. This study applies specialised mathematical and computational estimation approaches to the problem of sampling and discovering species of phages in microbiological communities. Our goal is to predict how many new phage species will be discovered in future samples based on species recorded in a phage sequence database for eight common bacterial hosts. While internal validation shows that current estimators yield low prediction errors for how many new species can be found when sampling more phages, these critically assume that the underlying mechanisms of phage sampling and database inclusion remain similar across time - an assumption put into question by our analysis for all but the host genus Mycobacterium. From this, we discuss how our results can still inform future sampling strategies - both through directly predicting the number of additional species, or by detecting changes in sampling and database inclusion. Our results have the potential to inform and optimise the hunt for and isolation of novel of phages from the the natural environment.
