## 摘要
蜜蜂（Apis mellifera）的肠道微生物组在宿主健康中起着核心作用，但其在农业景观中的变异仍鲜为人知。本研究通过一项在加拿大从不列颠哥伦比亚省到魁北克省跨越多种农业生态系统的大规模田间调查，探讨了主要环境压力源（尤其是农药暴露和RNA病毒载量）如何塑造蜜蜂肠道微生物组。我们确定了特定细菌分类群与主要RNA病毒之间的一致关联，包括沙雷氏菌（Serratia marcescens）随SBV富集，以及Bombella intestini随BQCV减少。农药暴露同样与关键微生物分类群的可重复变化相关。这些发现共同揭示了相互作用的压力源共同塑造了蜜蜂肠道微生物组，并有助于预测农业生态系统中微生物组的反应。

亮点
确定了肠道细菌与主要蜜蜂RNA病毒（BQCV、SBV、LSV、IAPV）之间的独特关联
农药暴露与关键微生物分类群的可重复变化相关
病毒-农药联合效应形成协调聚类，可预测微生物组变异和特定细菌反应
综合建模表明，环境压力源可共同解释超出作物效应的微生物组结构

## Abstract
The honey bee (Apis mellifera) gut microbiome plays a central role in host health, yet its variation across agricultural landscapes remains poorly resolved. This study investigates how major environmental stressors, particularly pesticide exposure and RNA virus loadings, shape the honey bee gut microbiome in a large-scale field study conducted across Canada, spanning diverse agroecosystems from British Columbia to Quebec. We identify consistent associations between specific bacterial taxa and major RNA viruses, including enrichment of Serratia marcescens with SBV and depletion of Bombella intestini with BQCV. Pesticide exposure is likewise linked to reproducible shifts in key microbial taxa. Together, these findings reveal that interacting stressors jointly shape the bee gut microbiome and enable prediction of microbiome responses in agroecosystems.

HighlightsDistinct associations identified between gut bacteria and major bee RNA viruses (BQCV, SBV, LSV, IAPV)
Pesticide exposure is linked to reproducible shifts in key microbial taxa
Combined virus-pesticide effects form coordinated clusters that predict microbiome variation and specific bacterial responses
Integrated modeling demonstrates that environmental stressors can jointly explain microbiome structure beyond crop effects
